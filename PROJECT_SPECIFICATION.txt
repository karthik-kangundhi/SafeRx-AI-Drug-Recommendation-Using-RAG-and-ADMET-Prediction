============================================================
1. PROJECT PURPOSE
============================================================

SafeRx AI is an educational/research-oriented AI system that analyzes drugs and molecules using molecular descriptors, ADMET prediction, toxicity/risk assessment, biomedical evidence retrieval, and patient-specific ranking.

The main workflow should communicate this concept:

ENTER A DRUG
        ↓
MOLECULAR STRUCTURE ANALYSIS
        ↓
MOLECULAR DESCRIPTORS
        ↓
ADMET PREDICTION
        ↓
TOXICITY / SAFETY RISK ASSESSMENT
        ↓
BIOMEDICAL EVIDENCE RETRIEVAL
        ↓
PATIENT-SPECIFIC SAFETY CHECK
        ↓
PERSONALIZED DRUG RANKING
        ↓
EXPLAINABLE RECOMMENDATION
        ↓
PDF REPORT

The project must clearly communicate:

“Enter a drug → SafeRx analyzes molecular properties → predicts ADMET endpoints → evaluates safety risks → retrieves supporting evidence → produces an explainable safety assessment.”

This is a research/educational decision-support prototype.
It must NOT claim to provide medical diagnosis or a clinical prescription.
